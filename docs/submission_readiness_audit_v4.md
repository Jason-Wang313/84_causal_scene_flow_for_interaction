## Ablation Failure

The full structured causal model is contradicted by a central ablation.

- Full causal scene-flow model: `0.76087 +/- 0.01126` mask F1.
- Correlation-only causal head: `0.78781 +/- 0.01137` mask F1.

The structured causal decomposition is therefore not supported.

## Submission Readiness Blockers

- The proposed model loses to the learned correlation classifier on the primary mask-F1 metric.
- The correlation-only ablation improves over the full proposed mechanism.
- Local synthetic evidence only; no real robot, high-fidelity simulator, or accepted external benchmark.

## Revival Requirements

- Evaluate on real RGB-D robot interaction data or an accepted high-fidelity scene-flow manipulation benchmark.
- Train the causal scene-flow model from intervention-labeled data.
- Compare against modern scene-flow, flow-matching, manipulation-world-model, and action-conditioned perception baselines.
- Demonstrate decisive paired gains in both causal attribution and downstream manipulation safety.
